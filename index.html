The Finding That Matters Most
Two different phone numbers on one page
Displayed on screen: 509-406-7378
Wired into the tap-to-call link: tel:+1 509 248 2530

509-248-2530 is the number from the earlier era of this business — still listed on the old GuildQuality profile alongside the allcitiessolar.com domain.

A homeowner reads one number and, if they tap rather than type, dials another. Some of those calls connect somewhere you are not, and you would never know they happened.

And there is no form. Solar gets researched in the evening. Someone comparing quotes at nine at night can do nothing on your site but write a number down and intend to ring tomorrow.

Your Market — Why This Year Is Not Your Fault

The One Big Beautiful Bill, signed 4 July 2025, terminated Section 25D — the 30% residential clean energy credit — for any system placed in service after 31 December 2025. Not reduced. Ended. Every residential quote you write in 2026 is effectively 30% more expensive to the customer, and Q4 2025 pulled a chunk of this year's demand forward on top. This is industry-wide. You were not outcompeted.

Worth checking before the call: commercial solar did not end the same way — those credits ran on different rules. You already sell commercial, battery storage and electrical work. That may be the only part of your business with a federal incentive still attached. Confirm the current position with your accountant.

And one fact that belongs on your website: Yakima gets over 152 sunny days a year and averages 6–7 hours of usable sunlight daily. With the credit gone, the argument shifts from "there's 30% off" to "your roof actually produces". That number is your argument and it is nowhere on the site.
