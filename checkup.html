The three things to fix first
1. Search engines and AI assistants are blocked

Read from allcitiessolar.net/robots.txt on 20 October, and again pretending to be Google’s own crawler. Same answer both times.

# allcitiessolar.net/robots.txt, read 20 October 2026 (end of file)
User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: Google-Extended
User-agent: AdsBot-Google
... 29 named crawlers ...
User-agent: *
Disallow: /

What it means: the last two lines apply to everyone not named above them, which includes Googlebot and Bing. Google can still show a bare link it already knew about, but it cannot read your pages, so it cannot rank them for “off-grid solar Yakima” or anything else.

It also blocks AdsBot-Google, the crawler Google uses to check ad landing pages. If ads pointed at this site today, Google could not check the page they land on.

The fix: in Squarespace, the site’s crawler and search engine settings. Minutes, once we have the login.

2. Every phone button dials your old number

All 15 pages show (509) 406-7378. The tap-to-call link behind it on every page is (509) 248-2530. Directories that copied your site (EnergySage, for one) also list 248-2530.

3. The site still sells the credit that ended

Your Residential page says “Solar is more affordable than ever with the Federal Tax credit of 30%!” and your FAQ says the credit “currently covers 30%”. The homeowner credit ended for systems placed in service after 2025. Your Government Incentives page, plus four more pages (Other Services and three EG4 pages), still show Squarespace demo text.

Good news on the same subject: the banner at the top of your homepage already says the business credit runs “until 2027”, which is in line with the deadlines in the July 2025 federal law change. Your homepage is more up to date than some of our own earlier pages were, which we have now corrected. Confirm the exact wording with your CPA.

SEO: can people find you on Google?
What we tested on 20 October

Three searches, run through a web search tool: “solar installer Yakima WA”, “off grid solar battery backup installer Yakima Valley” and your business name.

  • Your own website did not come up in any of the three.
  • What came up instead were directories and lists: EnergySage, EcoWatch, SolarReviews, Birdeye, ENF Solar and Solar Power World. Several list you, mostly with the old 2008 S 1st St address or the old phone number.
  • Competitors that did come up by name: Solora Solar, Core Northwest, Forecast Energy.

A search tool is not a Google ranking report. It shows what is being found; it does not give you a position.

What we would do
  • Day one: unblock search engines, fix the phone links, take down the five demo-text pages
  • One name, one address (2014 S 1st St), one phone number, everywhere: site, markup, Google Business Profile, and the directories listed above
  • A page for each service (batteries and off-grid, wind, farms and business, repairs) and for each of your five counties. The demo shows them
  • Your job photos and real reviews on the pages people land on
AEO: do you show up when people ask ChatGPT or Google’s AI?
Blocked, and answered from second-hand data

Your robots.txt names GPTBot (ChatGPT), Google-Extended (Google’s AI), ClaudeBot, Applebot-Extended, Meta-ExternalAgent and 24 others, and then blocks everyone else as well.

So when an AI assistant answers a question about you, it cannot use your own site. The search tool we used writes an AI summary, and for “off grid solar battery backup installer Yakima Valley” it said your phone number on EnergySage is (509) 248-2530 and that your website information is limited. That is the old number, coming from a directory.

What we would do
  • Allow the AI crawlers you want answering for you (a choice you make once)
  • Write the pages AI assistants quote: plain answers to the questions people ask, like “can I go off-grid in Klickitat County?” or “does solar still pay in Yakima in 2026?”
  • Fix your details in the directories AI tools lean on, starting with EnergySage and SolarReviews
