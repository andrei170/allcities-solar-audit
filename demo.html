Straight talk

Does solar still make sense without the 30%?

Sometimes yes. Sometimes no. We will tell you which.

What changed: the federal residential clean energy credit — Section 25D — ended for systems placed in service after 31 December 2025. There is no 30% federal credit on a residential system in 2026.

What did not change: your power bill, net metering with your utility, the cost of equipment, and the fact that a system you own outright still produces free electricity for twenty-five years.

What that means in practice: the payback period is longer than it was in 2025. For some houses it still works comfortably. For others it now does not, and we would rather tell you that on the phone than sell you a system you regret.

Commercial solar sits under different rules again, so if this is for a business the picture may be better than you expect. Ask us and we will walk you through it.
